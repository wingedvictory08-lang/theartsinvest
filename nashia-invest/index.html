Studio Acquisitions

Studio Acquisitions is an independent curatorial practice. I source, acquire, and curate fine art, vintage fashion, and jewelry. And when the project calls for it, the spaces they belong in.

My work's argument is swift: taste is a form of self-knowledge and perspective, and acquisition without it is aimless. Private sourcing, curatorial consultations, editorial writing, and spatial projects all draw from the same practice — knowing what you're searching for, researching the market, and identifying its value.
