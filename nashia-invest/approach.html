Approach

01

Start with the question.

Curation starts with a brief, not a shopping list. What is missing, what matters, and what should this work do in the room or collection?

02

Research before recommendation.

I look across galleries, auction and resale markets, vintage dealers, and private sellers. Comparables, condition, provenance where available, and context shape each recommendation.

03

Make a considered selection.

The goal is not to acquire more, but to find the right work, object, or relationship for the person, brand, or space it is meant to serve.

Taste is a form of self-knowledge and perspective.
